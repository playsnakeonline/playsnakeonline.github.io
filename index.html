    Gameplay

    The objective is simple: control the snake, eat food to increase its length, and survive as long as possible. As the snake grows, movement becomes more challenging, requiring careful turns and quick reactions. With improved mechanics and a clean design, the game keeps the original concept while making it more responsive and engaging for modern players.

    How to Play Google Snake

    Google Snake is a modern version of the classic snake game, keeping the same core idea with updated visuals and extra modes. You control a snake that grows longer as it eats food, making movement more challenging over time.

    • Basic Gameplay

    Guide the snake to collect food (such as apples) that appears on the screen. Each item you eat increases your length. The game ends if you hit the wall or collide with your own body, so careful movement is essential.

    • Controls

    Arrow Keys – Move the snake on desktop
    Swipe – Control direction on mobile devices

    • Objective

    Grow the snake as long as possible without crashing. The longer you survive, the higher your score.

    • Advanced Play

    As the snake grows, you’ll need better planning and positioning to avoid trapping yourself. Some versions also include extra modes with obstacles, portals, or special challenges that change how you play.

    Can You Win?

    Yes, it’s possible to fill the entire board by guiding the snake without making mistakes. This requires following a consistent movement pattern and leaving space to move until the grid is fully covered.

    Additional Features

    Modern versions may include different modes, customization options, and special challenges that add variety beyond the classic gameplay.
